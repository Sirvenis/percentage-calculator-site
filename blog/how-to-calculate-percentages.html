How to Calculate Percentages: Complete Beginner's Guide

Published: April 2026 | Reading time: 7 min

Percentages are everywhere — from restaurant tips and sale discounts to test scores and financial returns. Yet many people struggle with percentage calculations. This guide breaks down every type of percentage calculation you'll ever need.

What Is a Percentage?

The word "percent" literally means "per hundred." A percentage is a way to express a number as a fraction of 100. So 25% means 25 out of 100, or 25/100, which equals 0.25 as a decimal.

Basic Formula

The fundamental percentage formula is: Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

For example, if you got 45 out of 60 questions correct on a test: (45 / 60) × 100 = 75%

Finding a Percentage of a Number

To find X% of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. Example: What is 20% of 150? 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30. Or convert to decimal: 150 × 0.20 = 30.

Finding What Percentage One Number Is of Another

To find what percentage X is of Y, divide X by Y and multiply by 100. Example: 30 is what percentage of 150? (30 / 150) × 100 = 20%

Percentage Increase

Formula: ((New - Original) / Original) × 100. Example: A price went from $80 to $100. ((100-80)/80) × 100 = 25% increase.

Percentage Decrease

Same formula applies. Example: A price dropped from $120 to $90. ((120-90)/120) × 100 = 25% decrease.

Real-World Applications

  • Tips: 20% tip on a $65 dinner = $65 × 0.20 = $13
  • Discounts: 30% off a $200 item = $200 × 0.30 = $60 savings, final price $140
  • Tax: 8% sales tax on a $50 purchase = $50 × 0.08 = $4 tax
  • Grades: 17 out of 20 correct = (17/20) × 100 = 85%

Quick Tips

  • 10% is easy: just move the decimal one place left ($80 → $8)
  • 5% is half of 10%
  • 15% is 10% + 5%
  • 25% is the same as dividing by 4
  • 50% is the same as dividing by 2
