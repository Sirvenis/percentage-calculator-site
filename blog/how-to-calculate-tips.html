How to Calculate Restaurant Tips: Quick Mental Math Guide

Published: April 2026 | Reading time: 5 min

Tipping at restaurants is expected in many countries, but calculating the right amount in your head can be stressful. Here's a simple system that makes tip calculation effortless.

The 10% Trick

Nearly every tip calculation starts with finding 10%. To find 10% of any number, just move the decimal point one place to the left. Examples:

  • $45.00 → 10% = $4.50
  • $87.50 → 10% = $8.75
  • $124.00 → 10% = $12.40

Building Any Tip from 10%

Once you have 10%, you can calculate any tip:

  • 15% tip: 10% + half of 10%. ($4.50 + $2.25 = $6.75 on a $45 bill)
  • 18% tip: 10% + 8% (approximately double 4%, which is half of 8%)
  • 20% tip: Just double 10%. ($4.50 × 2 = $9.00 on a $45 bill)
  • 25% tip: 10% × 2.5, or simply divide the bill by 4. ($45 ÷ 4 = $11.25)

Splitting the Bill

When splitting a bill with tip among multiple people: (Bill + Tip) ÷ Number of people. Example: $120 bill with 20% tip among 4 people: ($120 + $24) ÷ 4 = $36 each.
